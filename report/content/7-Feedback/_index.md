# 7. Reflections & Feedback on the FCAJ Program — AWS Vietnam

**Student:** Do Tran Minh Phuc  
**University:** VNU-HCM University of Science (HCMUS)  
**Internship Organization:** Amazon Web Services Vietnam LLC (Bitexco Office, HCMC)

---

## 1. Workplace Experience at AWS Vietnam

My internship on the **26th Floor of the Bitexco Financial Tower** offered an invaluable, world-class professional working experience:
* **Inspiring Work Environment**: Modern, transparent, and collaborative workplace infrastructure fostering creativity and high-impact engineering.
* **Amazon "Day 1" Culture**: Immersed in core Amazon Leadership Principles: *Customer Obsession*, *Ownership*, *Bias for Action*, and *Insist on the Highest Standards*.

---

## 2. Mentorship & Guidance

I express my deepest gratitude to:
* **AWS Solutions Architects & Mentors**: For their continuous support, challenging architectural critiques, and guidance on architecting scalable Cloud-Native solutions with strict FinOps rigor.
* **The FCAJ Program Team**: For organizing high-impact learning initiatives (Community Days, Immersion Days) and providing guidance throughout the 12-week internship.

---

## 3. Constructive Suggestions for Future Cohorts

To further amplify the impact of the **First Cloud AI Journey** program:
1. **Incorporate Hands-on Mini Hackathons**: Introduce 24-hour team hackathons tackling real-world customer scenarios to reinforce collaborative cloud development skills.
2. **Expanded GenAI Sandbox Quotas**: Provide broader experimental sandbox access to cutting-edge Amazon Bedrock foundation models for advanced research prototypes.

---

## 4. Satisfaction Level

**Overall Satisfaction: 9/10** — The FCAJ program exceeded my expectations in terms of technical mentorship depth, real-world AWS exposure, and professional development opportunities at the Bitexco Financial Tower.

---

## 5. Would I Recommend This Program?

**Absolutely yes.** I would enthusiastically recommend the First Cloud AI Journey program to any university student interested in Cloud Computing and AI/ML. The program uniquely bridges the gap between academic theory and production-grade cloud engineering, providing hands-on experience with enterprise AWS services that are rarely accessible in university labs.

---

## 6. Conclusion

The **FCAJ Internship** served as a transformative bridge connecting my academic foundations at HCMUS with the rigorous standards of a professional Cloud Solutions Architect. I am deeply honored to have been part of this journey! 🚀
